# Summary

**Article 56** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 mandates the European AI Office to facilitate the drawing up of codes of practice at Union level to contribute to the proper application of obligations for general-purpose AI (GPAI) model providers[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Scope of the Code of Practice
1. **Baseline Obligations**: Detailing technical documentation formats (Annex XI), information to downstream providers (Annex XII), and copyright compliance policies.
2. **Systemic Risk Mitigation**: Operationalizing red-teaming, model evaluations, and cybersecurity controls for models trained with $>10^{25}$ FLOPs.
3. **Legal Effect**: Adherence to an approved Code of Practice confers a presumption of compliance with Articles 53 and 55.
