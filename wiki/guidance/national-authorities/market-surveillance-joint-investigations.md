# Summary

Articles 74 through 76 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 govern the operational cooperation of national **Market Surveillance Authorities (MSAs)** and their joint enforcement mechanisms[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Powers of Market Surveillance Authorities
- Unannounced on-site inspections and remote testing of AI systems.
- Power to compel access to training datasets, source code, and model weights where non-compliance poses severe systemic risks.
- Establishment of priority **AI Regulatory Sandboxes** (Article 57) to foster innovation while ensuring safety.
