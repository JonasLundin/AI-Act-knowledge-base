# Summary

Under **Article 96(1)**, the European Commission issues practical guidance regarding the interpretation and application of **Article 5 (Prohibited AI practices)**[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Key Interpretative Guidance
- Clarifies the threshold of 'significant harm' in subliminal manipulation and vulnerability exploitation.
- Details the practical demarcation between permitted biometrics in public health and prohibited biometric social scoring.
- Provides concrete case studies for law enforcement remote biometric identification authorizations.
