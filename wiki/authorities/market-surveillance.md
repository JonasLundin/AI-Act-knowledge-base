# Summary

**Market Surveillance Authorities (MSAs)** are designated by each Member State pursuant to **Article 70** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] to supervise and enforce AI Act compliance in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2019/1020.

# Powers and Responsibilities
- **Investigative Powers (Article 77)**: Full access to technical documentation, datasets, algorithmic models, and source code upon reasoned request.
- **Corrective Measures (Article 79)**: Power to order operators to bring non-compliant AI systems into conformity, withdraw them from the market, or recall them.
- **Cross-Border Mutual Assistance (Article 76)**: Conducting joint investigations and sharing information via the Information and Communication System for Market Surveillance (ICSMS).
- **Fines (Article 99)**: Imposing administrative fines up to €35M / 7% of worldwide turnover.
