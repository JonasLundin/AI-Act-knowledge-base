# Summary

**Notified Bodies** are independent conformity assessment bodies designated and notified by national notifying authorities under Chapter III, Section 4 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Roles and Governance
- **Conformity Assessments**: Conduct third-party audits of quality management systems and technical documentation for high-risk AI systems (Annex VII).
- **Notification in NANDO**: Formally notified by Member States via the Commission's NANDO information system (New Approach Notified and Designated Organisations).
- **Coordination Group (Article 39)**: Participate in the sectoral coordination group of notified bodies to establish harmonised assessment practices.
