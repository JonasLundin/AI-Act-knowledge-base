# Summary

**Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (MDR)**[^mdr-regulation-eu-2017-745] and **Regulation (EU) 2017/746 (IVDR)** govern medical devices and in vitro diagnostics. They are listed in **Annex I, Section A, points 11 and 12** of the AI Act[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Interplay with the AI Act
- **Article 6(1) High-Risk Qualification**: Medical device software (SaMD) classified in Risk Class IIa, IIb, or III under MDR requires notified body assessment and is therefore classified as high-risk under Article 6(1).
- **Application Timeline**: Applies from **2 August 2028** (Article 113(d) as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744).
- **Joint Conformity Assessment**: Notified bodies designated under MDR/IVDR that also obtain AI Act notification evaluate medical AI systems in a consolidated audit.
