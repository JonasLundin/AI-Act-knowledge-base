# Summary

**Regulation (EU) 2023/1230** on machinery[^machinery-regulation-eu-2023-1230] addresses risks arising from self-evolving AI and autonomous machinery. It is listed in **Annex I, Section A, point 1** of the AI Act[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Interplay with the AI Act
- **Article 6(1) Gateway**: An AI system that is a safety component of machinery subject to third-party conformity assessment under Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 is automatically high-risk under Article 6(1).
- **Deferred Application Date**: Pursuant to Article 113(d) as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, this Annex I gateway applies from **2 August 2028**.
- **Coordinated Conformity Assessment**: Article 43(3) allows the provider to integrate AI Act conformity assessments directly into the machinery conformity assessment procedure.
