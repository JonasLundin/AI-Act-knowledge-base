# Related EU Legislation

EU legal instruments that interact directly with Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act).

## Concepts

- [GDPR Interplay](gdpr-interplay.md) — Overlap between the AI Act and Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), including legal bases, automated decision-making, and DPIAs.
- [Cyber Resilience Act Interplay (CRA & Article 15(4))](cra-article-12.md) — Interplay with Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 on horizontal cybersecurity requirements and presumption of conformity.
- [Revised Product Liability Directive (Directive (EU) 2024/2853)](product-liability-directive-2024-2853.md) — Strict liability for defective software and AI systems, disclosure of evidence, and rebuttable presumptions.
- [Machinery Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2023/1230)](machinery-regulation-2023-1230.md) — Interplay regarding AI systems functioning as safety components in industrial machinery.
- [Medical Devices Regulations (MDR & IVDR)](mdr-ivdr.md) — Interplay with Regulations (EU) 2017/745 and (EU) 2017/746 regarding medical software and joint conformity assessments.
- [New Legislative Framework (NLF)](new-legislative-framework.md) — Foundational Union product harmonisation architecture under Decision 768/2008/EC and Regulation (EC) 765/2008.
