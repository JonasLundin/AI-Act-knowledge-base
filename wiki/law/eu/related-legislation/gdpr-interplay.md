# Summary

The AI Act operates without prejudice to Union data protection legislation, specifically **Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)** and Directive (EU) 2016/680[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Harmonised Application Principles
- **Cumulative Compliance**: Compliance with the AI Act does not exempt economic operators from GDPR lawful basis, data minimization, or data subject rights.
- **Article 22 GDPR (Automated Decision-Making)**: AI deployers must respect the right of data subjects not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing.
- **DPIA & FRIA Alignment**: A Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment under AI Act Article 27 may build upon and integrate with a GDPR Article 35 Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA).
