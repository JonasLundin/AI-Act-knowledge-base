# Summary

The **New Legislative Framework (NLF)** established by Decision No 768/2008/EC[^nlf-decision-768-2008-ec] and Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 provides the structural blueprint for EU product harmonisation legislation, upon which the AI Act[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] is constructed.

# Core Structural Elements Adopted in the AI Act
- **Economic Operator Chain**: Standardised obligations for providers (manufacturers), authorised representatives, importers, and distributors.
- **Conformity Assessment Modules**: Internal control (Module A / Annex VI) and Quality Management System verification (Module H / Annex VII).
- **CE Marking**: Affixing the CE mark as the visible proof of conformity with all applicable Union product directives.
- **Accreditation and Notification**: Rules for notifying authorities and conformity assessment bodies.
