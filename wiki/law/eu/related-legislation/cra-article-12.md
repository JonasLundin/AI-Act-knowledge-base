# Summary

The **Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)**, Regulation (EU) 2024/2847[^cra-regulation-eu-2024-2847], sets horizontal cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements. **Article 15(4)** of the AI Act[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] coordinates cybersecurity compliance between the two instruments.

For comprehensive coverage of CRA essential cybersecurity requirements, vulnerability handling, and CE marking, see the [Cyber Resilience Act Knowledge Base](https://github.com/JonasLundin/CRA-knowledge-base).

# Key Alignment Mechanisms
- **Presumption of Conformity**: Under Article 15(4) and Article 42(2) of the AI Act, high-risk AI systems that fulfill the essential cybersecurity requirements of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 are deemed to comply with the cybersecurity requirements set out in Article 15 of the AI Act.
- **Single Technical Documentation**: Providers can integrate CRA technical documentation and AI Act Annex IV technical documentation into a single unified dossier.
- **Vulnerability Handling**: Serious security incidents and actively exploited vulnerabilities fall under coordinated notification procedures between CSIRTs/ENISA under CRA and market surveillance authorities under the AI Act.
