# Summary

**Annex III (High-risk AI systems referred to in Article 6(2))** enumerates the eight standalone application domains where AI systems pose significant risks to fundamental rights, health, or safety[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

AI systems deployed in these domains must comply with all Chapter III mandatory requirements and undergo conformity assessment before entering the Union market.

# Complete Taxonomy of the 8 Annex III High-Risk Domains

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               ANNEX III STANDALONE HIGH-RISK DOMAINS              |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. BIOMETRICS                                                     |
|    - Remote biometric identification systems (post-RBI).          |
|    - Biometric categorization (inferring protected attributes).   |
|    - Emotion recognition systems.                                 |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE                                        |
|    - Safety components in digital infrastructure, road traffic,   |
|      water, gas, heating, and electricity supply.                 |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. EDUCATION & VOCATIONAL TRAINING                                |
|    - Admission/assignment to educational institutions.            |
|    - Evaluating learning outcomes and steering student paths.     |
|    - Monitoring/detecting student behavior during examinations.  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4. EMPLOYMENT & WORKER MANAGEMENT                                 |
|    - Recruitment, screening applications, evaluating candidates.  |
|    - Promotion, termination, task allocation, and worker tracking.|
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 5. ESSENTIAL PUBLIC & PRIVATE SERVICES                            |
|    - Evaluating eligibility for public assistance / social welfare|
|    - Creditworthiness scoring and credit assessment.              |
|    - Risk assessment and pricing for life & health insurance.     |
|    - Emergency call dispatch and triaging emergency services.     |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 6. LAW ENFORCEMENT                                                |
|    - Assessing individual risk of offending/re-offending.         |
|    - Polygraphs and lie detection tools.                          |
|    - Evaluating reliability of evidence during investigations.    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 7. MIGRATION, ASYLUM & BORDER CONTROL                             |
|    - Polygraphs in border examination.                            |
|    - Assessing security risks of asylum seekers / visa applicants.|
|    - Examination of asylum and residence applications.            |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 8. ADMINISTRATION OF JUSTICE & DEMOCRATIC PROCESSES               |
|    - Assisting judicial authorities in interpreting law and facts.|
|    - AI systems intended to influence the outcome of elections.   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
```
