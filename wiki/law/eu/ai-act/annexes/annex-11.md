**Annex XI: Technical Documentation Referred to in Article 53(1), Point (a) — Technical Documentation for Providers of General-Purpose AI Models** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Substantive Scope

Specifies the technical documentation required for providers of general-purpose AI models under Article 53(1)(a), including training methodology, dataset curation, computational resources, and energy consumption.
