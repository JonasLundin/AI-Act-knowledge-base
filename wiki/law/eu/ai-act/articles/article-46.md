**Article 46: Derogation from conformity assessment procedure** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Legal Scope and Substance

Authorises national market surveillance authorities to exceptionally permit placing on the market of specific high-risk AI systems for urgent public security or health reasons.
