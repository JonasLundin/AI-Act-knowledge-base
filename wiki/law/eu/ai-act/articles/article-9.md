# Summary

**Article 9 (Risk management system)** establishes the foundational requirement for high-risk AI systems under Chapter III of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

Providers must establish, implement, document, and maintain a continuous, iterative risk management system that operates across the entire lifecycle of the high-risk AI system.

# Mandatory Risk Management Phases (Article 9(2))

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               ARTICLE 9 RISK MANAGEMENT LIFECYCLE                 |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. IDENTIFICATION & ANALYSIS (Point a)                            |
|    - Identify and evaluate known and reasonably foreseeable risks |
|      to health, safety, or fundamental rights.                    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. ESTIMATION & EVALUATION (Point b)                              |
|    - Estimate and evaluate risks that may emerge when the system  |
|      is used in accordance with intended purpose or reasonably    |
|      foreseeable misuse.                                          |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. POST-MARKET FEEDBACK EVALUATION (Point c)                      |
|    - Evaluate other risks based on analysis of data gathered from |
|      the post-market monitoring system.                           |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4. ADOPTION OF TARGETED MITIGATION MEASURES (Point d)             |
|    - Adopt appropriate and targeted risk mitigation measures      |
|      following the mandatory elimination hierarchy.              |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

# Hierarchy of Risk Mitigation Measures (Article 9(4))
When designing risk mitigation measures, providers must adhere to this mandatory priority order:
1. **Design & Development Elimination**: Eliminate or reduce risks as far as possible through inherently safe system design and development;
2. **Mitigation Controls**: Implement adequate technical mitigation and protection measures regarding risks that cannot be eliminated;
3. **Information & Training**: Provide clear warnings, technical documentation, and training instructions to deployers regarding residual risks.

# Pre-Market and Post-Market Testing (Article 9(5)–(8))
- High-risk AI systems must be tested against predefined metrics and probabilistic performance thresholds.
- Testing must evaluate performance on representative datasets before placement on the market and under simulated real-world conditions.
