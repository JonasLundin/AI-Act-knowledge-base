# Summary

**Article 5 (Prohibited AI Practices)** defines the AI Act's unconditional prohibitions[^regulation-eu-2024-1689]. AI systems that fall within Article 5 are deemed to create unacceptable risks to human health, safety, and fundamental rights and may not be placed on the market, put into service, or used anywhere in the European Union.

Infringements of Article 5 trigger the maximum administrative fines under Article 99(3): up to **€35,000,000 or 7% of total worldwide annual turnover**.

# The 10 Prohibited AI Practices

Following amendments by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI)[^regulation-eu-2026-1744], Article 5 establishes 10 prohibited AI practices:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               ARTICLE 5 UNACCEPTABLE RISK PROHIBITIONS            |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. COGNITIVE BEHAVIORAL MANIPULATION (Article 5(1)(a))            |
|    - Deploying subliminal, purposeful manipulative or deceptive   |
|      techniques distorting behavior and causing significant harm. |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. EXPLOITATION OF VULNERABILITIES (Article 5(1)(b))              |
|    - Exploiting age, disability, or socio-economic situation to   |
|      substantially distort behavior and cause significant harm.   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. NON-CONSENSUAL INTIMATE IMAGERY (Article 5(1)(ba))             |
|    - Systems generating, manipulating, or spreading non-consensual|
|      sexually explicit deepfakes/intimate images (applies 2026-12)|
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4. CHILD SEXUAL ABUSE MATERIAL (CSAM) (Article 5(1)(bb))          |
|    - Systems generating, disseminating, or facilitating CSAM/CSAE |
|      material or child exploitation (applies 2026-12-02).         |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 5. SOCIAL SCORING (Article 5(1)(c))                               |
|    - Evaluation or classification of natural persons based on     |
|      social behavior leading to detrimental treatment across      |
|      unrelated contexts or disproportionate consequences.         |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 6. INDIVIDUAL PREDICTIVE POLICING (Article 5(1)(d))               |
|    - Assessing criminal offence risk based solely on profiling or |
|      personality traits without objective corroborating facts.    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 7. UNTARGETED FACIAL SCRAPING (Article 5(1)(e))                   |
|    - Untargeted scraping of facial images from the internet or    |
|      CCTV footage to build or expand facial recognition databases.|
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 8. EMOTION RECOGNITION AT WORK & SCHOOL (Article 5(1)(f))         |
|    - Emotion recognition systems in workplace or educational      |
|      institutions (except medical or safety purposes).            |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 9. BIOMETRIC CATEGORISATION FOR SENSITIVE TRAITS (Article 5(1)(g))|
|    - Biometric categorization deducing race, political opinions,  |
|      trade union membership, religion, philosophical beliefs, or  |
|      sexual orientation (except lawful law enforcement filtering).|
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 10. REAL-TIME PUBLIC REMOTE BIOMETRICS (Article 5(1)(h))          |
|    - 'Real-time' remote biometric identification in public spaces |
|      for law enforcement, subject to narrow judicial exceptions.  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

# Application Dates
- Prohibitions (a) through (h) apply from **2 February 2025**.
- Prohibitions (ba) and (bb) inserted by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 apply from **2 December 2026** alongside supplementary enforcement procedures under paragraphs 1a and 1b.
