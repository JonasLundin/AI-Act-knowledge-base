# Summary

**Article 112: Evaluation and review** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

Legal provisions and requirements of Article 112 (Evaluation and review) under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act).

# Legal Scope and Substance

Establishes a mandatory review cycle under which the Commission submits comprehensive evaluation reports on the AI Act to the European Parliament and Council every four years (and reviews of Article 5 and Annex III every two years).
