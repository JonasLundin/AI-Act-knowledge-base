# Summary

**Article 99 (Penalties)** sets forth the financial sanctions and administrative fine ceilings that national market surveillance authorities and the European AI Office can impose for infringements of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

The fine structure exceeds even the EU GDPR, creating the world's most severe regulatory financial liabilities for non-compliant AI.

# The Three Statutory Fine Tiers

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               ARTICLE 99 ADMINISTRATIVE FINE TIERS                |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TIER 1: PROHIBITED AI PRACTICES (Article 99(3))                   |
| - Infringement of Article 5 (Prohibited practices)                |
| - Maximum Fine: Up to €35,000,000 or 7% of total worldwide       |
|   annual turnover of the preceding financial year.                |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TIER 2: HIGH-RISK AI & OPERATOR OBLIGATIONS (Article 99(4))       |
| - Non-compliance with Chapter III obligations (Articles 9–15, 16) |
| - Maximum Fine: Up to €15,000,000 or 3% of total worldwide       |
|   annual turnover of the preceding financial year.                |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TIER 3: INCORRECT OR MISLEADING INFORMATION (Article 99(5))       |
| - Supplying incorrect, incomplete, or misleading information to   |
|   notified bodies or market surveillance authorities.             |
| - Maximum Fine: Up to €7,500,000 or 1.5% of total worldwide       |
|   annual turnover of the preceding financial year.                |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

# Fines for Providers of GPAI Models (Article 101)
For providers of General-Purpose AI models, the European Commission may impose fines up to:
- **€15,000,000** or **3% of total worldwide annual turnover**.

# Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) & Startups (Article 99(6))
For SMEs and startups, the fines must be the *lower* of the two amounts (fixed euro amount vs. percentage of turnover), preventing punitive bankruptcy for emerging innovators.
