# Summary

**Article 99 (Penalties)** establishes the framework for administrative fines and sanctions under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689[^regulation-eu-2024-1689]. Administrative fines under Article 99 are imposed by **Member States** through their designated national market surveillance authorities.

# The Three Administrative Fine Tiers

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               ARTICLE 99 ADMINISTRATIVE FINE CEILINGS             |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TIER 1: PROHIBITED AI INFRINGEMENTS (Article 99(3))               |
| Violation of Article 5 (unacceptable risk / banned practices):     |
| -> Up to €35,000,000 OR 7% of total worldwide annual turnover,    |
|    whichever is higher.                                           |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TIER 2: ENUMERATED OBLIGATIONS NON-COMPLIANCE (Article 99(4))     |
| Non-compliance with Articles 16, 22, 23, 24, 25 (point da), 26,    |
| 31, 33, 34, or 50:                                                |
| -> Up to €15,000,000 OR 3% of total worldwide annual turnover,    |
|    whichever is higher.                                           |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TIER 3: MISLEADING INFORMATION (Article 99(5))                    |
| Supplying incorrect, incomplete or misleading information to      |
| notified bodies or national competent authorities:                |
| -> Up to €7,500,000 OR 1% of total worldwide annual turnover,     |
|    whichever is higher.                                           |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

# Enumerated High-Risk Obligations (Article 99(4))
Article 99(4) specifically penalises breaches of:
- Article 16 (Provider obligations for high-risk AI);
- Article 22 (Authorised representatives);
- Article 23 (Importers);
- Article 24 (Distributors);
- Article 25 (Responsibilities along the AI value chain, added by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 point (da));
- Article 26 (Deployers of high-risk AI);
- Article 31 (Notification procedure);
- Article 33 (Notified body standards);
- Article 34 (Subcontracting by notified bodies);
- Article 50 (Transparency obligations).

# SME, Startup and Medium Enterprise Protections
- **SMEs and Startups (Article 99(6))**: For small and medium-sized enterprises (including start-ups), fines are capped at whichever amount is **lower** between the fixed sum and the turnover percentage.
- **Medium-Sized Enterprises (Article 99(6a))**: Inserted by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744[^regulation-eu-2026-1744], national authorities must take into account specific turnover structures and apply proportionate fine guidelines.
