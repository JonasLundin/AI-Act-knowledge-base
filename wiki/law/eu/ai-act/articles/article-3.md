# Summary

**Article 3 (Definitions)** provides the definitive legal taxonomy for Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

Harmonised with the OECD 2023 definition, Article 3 establishes the legal boundary distinguishing artificial intelligence systems from conventional rule-based or deterministic software.

# Core Statutory Definitions

### 1. AI System (Article 3(1))
> *"a machine-based system that is designed to operate with varying levels of autonomy and that may exhibit adaptiveness after deployment, and that, for explicit or implicit objectives, infers, from the input it receives, how to generate outputs such as predictions, content, recommendations, or decisions that can influence physical or virtual environments."*

Key criteria:
- **Autonomy**: Operates without constant human intervention.
- **Inference**: Infers outputs through learning, reasoning, or modeling (distinguishing it from classical sequential programming).
- **Influence**: Generates predictions, content, recommendations, or decisions.

### 2. General-Purpose AI (GPAI) Model (Article 3(63))
> *"an AI model, including where such an AI model is trained for a large amount of data using self-supervision at scale, that displays significant generality and is capable of competently performing a wide range of distinct tasks regardless of the way the model is placed on the market and that can be integrated into a variety of downstream systems or applications."*
*(Excludes AI models used before placement on the market for research, development, or prototyping).*

### 3. Key Economic Operator Roles
- **Provider (Article 3(2))**: Natural or legal person that develops an AI system or GPAI model, or has it developed, and places it on the market or puts it into service under its own name or trademark.
- **Deployer (Article 3(4))**: Any natural or legal person using an AI system under its authority (excluding personal non-professional use).
- **Authorized Representative (Article 3(5))**: Person established in the Union mandated by a non-EU provider.
- **Importer (Article 3(6))**: Established in the Union placing third-country AI systems on the EU market.
- **Distributor (Article 3(7))**: Any person in the supply chain other than the provider or importer making an AI system available.
