# Summary

**Article 111 (AI systems already placed on the market or put into service)** governs transitional arrangements and grandfathering mechanisms for pre-existing AI systems[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Transitional Regimes

1. **High-Risk AI Systems (Article 111(1))**:
   - AI systems that are components of large-scale IT systems established by legal acts in the Area of Freedom, Security and Justice (e.g. SIS II, VIS, Eurodac) placed on the market before 2 August 2027 must be brought into compliance by 31 December 2030.
2. **General Pre-existing High-Risk Systems (Article 111(2))**:
   - High-risk AI systems placed on the market or put into service before the applicable date of application continue to be subject to the legal regime under which they were placed on the market, unless they undergo significant changes in their design or intended purpose.
3. **General-Purpose AI Models (Article 111(3))**:
   - GPAI models placed on the market before 2 August 2025 must be brought into compliance with Chapter V obligations by 2 August 2027.
4. **Article 50 Transparency Reprieve (Article 111(4))**:
   - Inserted by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744[^regulation-eu-2026-1744]: AI systems placed on the market or put into service before 2 August 2026 are granted a transitional reprieve until **2 December 2026** to comply with the transparency and synthetic content watermarking obligations set out in Article 50.
