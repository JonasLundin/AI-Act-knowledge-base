# Summary

**Article 27 (Fundamental rights impact assessment for high-risk AI systems)** establishes a mandatory requirement for certain deployers to evaluate the specific societal and civil impacts of deploying a high-risk AI system prior to putting it into service[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Regulated Addressees
The FRIA requirement applies to:
- Deployers that are bodies governed by public law or private operators providing public services (e.g. public healthcare, social security, utilities);
- Deployers of high-risk AI systems in banking credit scoring and life/health insurance risk assessment.

# FRIA Assessment Components (Article 27(1))

1. **Clear Context Analysis**: Description of the deployer's processes and specific time period in which the system will be used.
2. **Impacted Groups**: Identification of specific categories of natural persons and vulnerable groups likely to be affected.
3. **Fundamental Rights Risks**: Assessment of specific risks of marginalization, discrimination, or infringement of rights (privacy, freedom of expression, non-discrimination).
4. **Human Oversight Measures**: Description of human oversight arrangements implemented within the deployer's operational workflows.
5. **Mitigation Plan**: Concrete measures to mitigate identified risks, including complaints mechanisms and redress channels.

# Notification to Market Surveillance
Deployers must submit the completed FRIA summary to the designated market surveillance authority and notify relevant national bodies.
