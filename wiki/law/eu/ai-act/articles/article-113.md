# Summary

**Article 113 (Entry into force and application)** establishes the phased chronological entry into application of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] across the European Union, incorporating the deferrals enacted by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI)[^regulation-eu-2026-1744].

# Consolidated Applicability Table

| Date | Subject Matter / Provisions in Scope | Legal Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **1 August 2024** | Entry into force of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. | Article 113 |
| **2 February 2025** | Chapter I (General Provisions) and Chapter II (Prohibited AI Practices, Article 5 points a to h). | Article 113(a) |
| **2 August 2025** | Chapter III Section 4 (Notifying Authorities), Chapter V (General-Purpose AI Models), Chapter VII (Governance), Chapter XII (Penalties, except Art 101), Article 78 (Confidentiality). | Article 113(b) |
| **27 July 2026** | Entry into force of Regulation (EU) 2026/1744; direct application of Articles 102 to 110. | Reg (EU) 2026/1744 |
| **2 August 2026** | General application of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, including Article 50 transparency requirements. | Article 113 |
| **2 December 2026** | Application of Article 5(1) points (ba) (intimate deepfakes) and (bb) (CSAM); expiry of Article 111(4) transparency reprieve for existing systems. | Reg (EU) 2026/1744 |
| **2 December 2027** | Stand-alone high-risk AI systems under Article 6(2) and Annex III (deferred from 2 August 2026). | Article 113(c), as amended |
| **2 August 2028** | High-risk AI systems that are safety components of Annex I products (deferred from 2 August 2027). | Article 113(d), as amended |
| **31 December 2030**| Compliance deadline for high-risk AI components of large-scale JHA IT systems under Article 111(1). | Article 111(1) |
