# AI Act

The regulation itself: articles, annexes, amendments and secondary legislation.

## Structure

- [Articles](articles/index.md): One page per article of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, consolidated with its amendments.
- [Annexes](annexes/index.md): One page per annex, including Annex I Union harmonisation legislation and Annex III high-risk areas.
- [Amendments](amendments/index.md): Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI) and later amending acts, with what each changed.
- [Secondary legislation](secondary-legislation/index.md): Delegated and implementing acts, Commission guidelines with legal effect, and a register of pending empowerments.
