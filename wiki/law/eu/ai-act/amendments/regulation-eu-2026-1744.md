# Summary

**Regulation (EU) 2026/1744** (the Digital Omnibus on AI)[^regulation-eu-2026-1744] amends Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689], Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 (EASA Basic Regulation), and Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 (Machinery Regulation). Published in the Official Journal on 24 July 2026, it entered into force on 27 July 2026.

ELI: [http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj](http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj)

# Substantive Amendments to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689

| Provision Amended | Nature of Amendment | Effective Application Date |
|---|---|---|
| **Article 5(1)** | Adds point (ba) prohibiting AI generating non-consensual sexually explicit deepfakes / intimate imagery, and point (bb) prohibiting AI generating or facilitating Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). | Applies from 2 December 2026. |
| **Article 5(1a), (1b)** | Inserts supplementary enforcement and procedural safeguards for the new prohibitions. | Applies from 2 December 2026. |
| **Article 6(1a)–(1c)** | Clarifies the boundaries of safety components under Union harmonisation legislation listed in Annex I and coordinates conformity assessment procedures with sector-specific Union acts. | Applies alongside respective Annex I timelines. |
| **Article 50(7)** | Adjusts specific marking and machine-readability exemptions for synthetic content. | Applies from 2 August 2026. |
| **Article 99(4)** | Inserts new point (da) adding Article 25 to the list of infringements punishable under the Tier 2 fine ceiling (€15M / 3%). | Applies from 2 August 2026. |
| **Article 99(6a)** | Introduces a dedicated turnover-proportionality cap for medium-sized enterprises to prevent disproportionate economic impacts. | Applies from 2 August 2026. |
| **Article 111(4)** | Grants a transitional reprieve until 2 December 2026 for AI systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 with respect to Article 50 transparency duties. | In force 27 July 2026. |
| **Article 113(c)** | Defers full application of Article 6(2) and Annex III standalone high-risk AI requirements from 2 August 2026 to **2 December 2027**. | In force 27 July 2026. |
| **Article 113(d)** | Defers application of Article 6(1) and Annex I high-risk safety components from 2 August 2027 to **2 August 2028**. | In force 27 July 2026. |
| **Articles 102–110** | Applies supervisory and investigatory provisions directly from 27 July 2026. | 27 July 2026. |
