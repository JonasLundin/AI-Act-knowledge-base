# Summary

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 includes dynamic delegated power clauses empowering the European Commission to update technical thresholds and application lists as AI capabilities evolve[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Delegated Powers
1. **Annex III Updates (Article 7)**: Adding or modifying high-risk AI use cases within existing critical domains.
2. **Systemic Risk Threshold (Article 51(3))**: Modifying the $10^{25}$ FLOPs cumulative computation threshold in light of algorithmic efficiencies and benchmark advances.
3. **Exercise of Delegation (Article 97)**: Conferral of powers for a period of five years, subject to European Parliament and Council scrutiny.
