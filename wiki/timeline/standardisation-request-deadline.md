# Summary

**28 February 2027**: The statutory deadline established by Commission Implementing Decision C(2025) 3871 (standardisation request M/613)[^commission-decision-c-2025-3871] for CEN and CENELEC to adopt and submit the ten mandated European harmonised standards supporting Regulation (EU) 2024/1689[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].
