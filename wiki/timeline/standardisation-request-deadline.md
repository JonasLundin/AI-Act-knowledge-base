# Summary

**30 April 2025**: Deadline for European Standardisation Organisations (CEN/CENELEC) to adopt and deliver initial harmonised European standards under Commission Standardisation Request C(2023) 3215[^commission-decision-c-2025-3871].

# Strategic Standardisation Context

The European Commission requested CEN and CENELEC to establish harmonised standards supporting the essential requirements for high-risk AI systems under Chapter III Section 2 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689[^regulation-eu-2024-1689]. Due to the complex scope covering risk management, data quality, record-keeping, transparency, human oversight, and cybersecurity, standardisation work continued under supplementary mandate C(2025) 3871, establishing the normative foundation for presumption of conformity.
