# Summary

**2 December 2027**: Application date for **Annex III standalone high-risk AI systems** (Article 6(2)) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689[^regulation-eu-2024-1689], deferred from the original 2 August 2026 date by **Regulation (EU) 2026/1744** (Digital Omnibus on AI)[^regulation-eu-2026-1744] to provide adequate time for CEN/CENELEC harmonised standards delivery and national market surveillance readiness.
