# AI Act Timeline & Transition Milestones

Chronological schedule of key implementation dates, staged applicability periods, and enforcement milestones under Article 113 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744.

## Concepts

- [AI Act Entry into Force](entry-into-force.md) — Entry into force of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 on 1 August 2024.
- [Prohibited Practices & AI Literacy Application](prohibited-practices-application.md) — Application of Article 5 prohibitions and Article 4 AI literacy on 2 February 2025.
- [GPAI Obligations & Governance Application](gpai-governance-application.md) — Application of Chapter V GPAI rules and Chapter VII governance infrastructure on 2 August 2025.
- [Digital Omnibus Regulation Entry into Force](omnibus-regulation-entry-into-force.md) — Entry into force of Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 on 27 July 2026.
- [Article 50 Transparency Obligations Application](article-50-transparency-application.md) — Transparency obligations application on 2 August 2026 (reprieve until 2 December 2026 under Article 111(4)).
- [Standardisation Request Delivery Deadline](standardisation-request-deadline.md) — Delivery deadline of 28 February 2027 under Decision C(2025) 3871 for CEN and CENELEC.
- [Annex III High-Risk Systems Application](high-risk-application.md) — Application to standalone high-risk systems under Annex III on 2 December 2027.
- [Annex I Product Safety Alignment Application](annex-i-products-application.md) — Application to high-risk systems embedded as safety components in Union product legislation on 2 August 2028.
- [Article 112 Review and Evaluation Milestones](article-112-review-evaluation.md) — Quadrennial evaluation reports by the Commission starting 2 August 2028.
