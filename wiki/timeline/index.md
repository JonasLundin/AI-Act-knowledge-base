# AI Act Timeline & Transition Milestones

Chronological schedule of key implementation dates, staged applicability periods, and enforcement milestones under Article 111.

## Concepts

- [Annex I Product Safety Alignment Application](annex-i-products-application.md) — Application to high-risk systems embedded as safety components in Union product legislation on 2 August 2027.
- [AI Act Entry into Force](entry-into-force.md) — Entry into force of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 on 1 August 2024.
- [GPAI Obligations & Governance Application](gpai-governance-application.md) — Application of Chapter V GPAI rules and Chapter VII governance infrastructure on 2 August 2025.
- [Annex III High-Risk Systems Application](high-risk-application.md) — Full application to high-risk systems under Annex III on 2 August 2026.
- [Prohibited Practices & AI Literacy Application](prohibited-practices-application.md) — Application of Article 5 prohibited practices and Article 4 AI literacy on 2 February 2025.
