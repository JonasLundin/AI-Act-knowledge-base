# Summary

**2 August 2026 / 2 December 2026**: General application of **Article 50 transparency obligations** (interaction disclosure, synthetic content watermarking, deepfake labeling, emotion recognition disclosure)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689]. Pursuant to **Article 111(4)** as inserted by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744[^regulation-eu-2026-1744], existing AI systems placed on the market prior to 2 August 2026 benefit from a 4-month transitional reprieve until **2 December 2026** to implement technical marking solutions.
