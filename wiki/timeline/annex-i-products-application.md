# Summary

**2 August 2028**: Application date for **Annex I embedded safety component high-risk AI systems** (Article 6(1)) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689[^regulation-eu-2024-1689], deferred from the original 2 August 2027 date by **Regulation (EU) 2026/1744** (Digital Omnibus on AI)[^regulation-eu-2026-1744] to synchronize with sectoral revisions across machinery, medical devices, civil aviation, and motor vehicles.
