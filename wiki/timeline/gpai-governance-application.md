# Summary

**2 August 2025**: General application of **Chapter V (General-Purpose AI Models)** and institutional governance structures under Article 113(b) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Scope of Chapter V Application

From this date, twelve months after entry into force, providers of general-purpose AI models placed on the Union market must comply with technical documentation duties, copyright policy obligations, and training data summaries under Article 53. Models designated as posing systemic risk must implement model evaluations and cybersecurity safeguards under Article 55, subject to direct supervision and fines enforced by the European AI Office.
