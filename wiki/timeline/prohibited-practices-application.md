# Summary

**2 February 2025**: Full statutory application of **Article 5 (Prohibited AI Practices)** and general principles under Article 113(a) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Enforcement and Legal Effect

Six months after entry into force, the EU-wide ban on unacceptable-risk AI systems took effect. The prohibited practices encompass social scoring, subliminal manipulation, exploitation of vulnerabilities, untargeted facial recognition scraping, biometric categorisation of sensitive attributes, and workplace/school emotion recognition. National market surveillance authorities are fully empowered to enforce prohibitions and impose tier-1 administrative fines of up to €35 million or 7% of annual worldwide turnover.
