# Summary

Under **Article 6(1)** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, an AI system is automatically classified as **high-risk** if it meets two cumulative criteria:
1. The AI system is intended to be used as a safety component of a product, or is itself a product, covered by the Union harmonisation legislation listed in Annex I; and
2. The product is required to undergo a third-party conformity assessment under that legislation[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Covered Annex I Sectors and Legislation
- Machinery (Regulation (EU) 2023/1230)
- Toys (Directive 2009/48/EC)
- Recreational craft (Directive 2013/53/EU)
- Lifts (Directive 2014/33/EU)
- Equipment for explosive atmospheres - ATEX (Directive 2014/34/EU)
- Radio equipment - RED (Directive 2014/53/EU)
- Pressure equipment (Directive 2014/68/EU)
- Cableway installations (Regulation (EU) 2016/424)
- Personal protective equipment - PPE (Regulation (EU) 2016/425)
- Medical devices - MDR (Regulation (EU) 2017/745)
- In vitro diagnostic medical devices - IVDR (Regulation (EU) 2017/746)
- Civil aviation safety (Regulation (EU) 2018/1139)
- Motor vehicles and trailers (Regulation (EU) 2019/2144)
