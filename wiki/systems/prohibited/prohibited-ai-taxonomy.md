# Summary

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 classifies AI systems according to a four-tier risk model. The highest tier comprises **Prohibited AI Systems**, whose commercial placement, putting into service, or use is strictly illegal within the Union[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744[^regulation-eu-2026-1744].

# The 10 Prohibited Categories
1. **Subliminal Distortion of Behavior**: Manipulating persons beyond their consciousness.
2. **Vulnerability Exploitation**: Exploiting vulnerabilities based on age, physical or mental disability, or socio-economic situation.
3. **Non-consensual Intimate Imagery**: Generating or spreading non-consensual intimate deepfakes (applies 2 December 2026).
4. **Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)**: Generating or facilitating CSAM (applies 2 December 2026).
5. **Biometric Social Scoring**: Evaluating individuals based on social conduct over time.
6. **Predictive Policing Profiling**: Assessing risk of individual criminal offending without objective verifiable facts.
7. **Facial Scraping Databases**: Untargeted scraping of internet or CCTV facial imagery.
8. **Workplace / School Emotion Recognition**: Detecting emotional states in employment or educational institutions.
9. **Biometric Categorization of Sensitive Attributes**: Deducing race, religion, sexual orientation from biometrics.
10. **Public Space Real-Time Biometrics**: Real-time remote biometric identification by police in publicly accessible spaces (with narrow judicial exceptions).
