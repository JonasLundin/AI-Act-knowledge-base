# Summary

Systems presenting specific **transparency risks** do not pose the severe threats of high-risk AI or banned systems, but risk deceiving, misleading, or manipulating citizens[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Taxonomy of Transparency-Risk Systems
1. **Conversational Agents & Chatbots**: Systems directly interacting with natural persons requiring disclosure of artificial nature (Article 50(1)).
2. **Generative AI Systems**: Synthetic audio, image, video, or text generators requiring machine-readable watermarking and detection markers (Article 50(2)).
3. **Emotion Recognition & Biometric Categorization**: Systems processing biometric indicators to infer emotional states or categorize individuals (Article 50(3)).
4. **Deepfake Generators & Synthetic Public Text**: Systems creating realistic manipulated media requiring prominent visible labelling (Article 50(4)).
