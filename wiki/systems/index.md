# Systems

The regulation's risk categories and the systems that fall into each.

## Sections

- [General-purpose AI models](general-purpose-ai/index.md) — GPAI models with and without systemic risk.
- [High-risk under Annex I](high-risk-annex-i/index.md) — AI as a safety component of, or as, a product covered by listed Union harmonisation legislation.
- [Annex III High-Risk AI Systems](high-risk-annex-iii/index.md) — The eight standalone application domains identified under Article 6(2) and Annex III of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 as posing high risks to fundamental rights, health, or safety.
- [Prohibited](prohibited/index.md) — Systems and practices banned by Article 5.
- [Transparency-risk](transparency-risk/index.md) — Systems with Article 50 duties only.

## Concepts

- [General-Purpose AI Models (GPAI)](general-purpose-ai-models.md) — Foundational AI models capable of performing a wide range of distinct tasks, including models with systemic risk (> 10^25 FLOPs).
- [Prohibited AI Practices (Article 5)](prohibited-ai-practices.md) — Unacceptable risk AI practices banned across the EU: cognitive manipulation, social scoring, biometric scraping, and real-time biometric identification.
