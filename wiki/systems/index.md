# Systems

The regulation's risk categories and the systems that fall into each.

## Structure

- [Prohibited](prohibited/index.md): Systems and practices banned by Article 5.
- [High-risk under Annex I](high-risk-annex-i/index.md): AI as a safety component of, or as, a product covered by listed Union harmonisation legislation.
- [High-risk under Annex III](high-risk-annex-iii/index.md): One page per Annex III area, with the Article 6(3) derogation.
- [Transparency-risk](transparency-risk/index.md): Systems with Article 50 duties only.
- [General-purpose AI models](general-purpose-ai/index.md): GPAI models with and without systemic risk.
