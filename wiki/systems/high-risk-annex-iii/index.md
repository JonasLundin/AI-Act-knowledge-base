# Annex III High-Risk AI Systems

The eight standalone application domains identified under Article 6(2) and Annex III of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 as posing high risks to fundamental rights, health, or safety.

## Concepts

- [High-Risk Area: Access to Essential Services](access-to-essential-services.md) — Eligibility evaluation for public assistance benefits, creditworthiness scoring, emergency service triage, and health/life insurance risk pricing.
- [High-Risk Area: Administration of Justice and Democratic Processes](administration-of-justice-and-democratic-processes.md) — Assisting judicial authorities in research and interpretation of law, and systems intended to influence election outcomes.
- [High-Risk Area: Biometrics](biometrics.md) — Remote biometric identification systems, biometric categorization, and emotion recognition systems.
- [High-Risk Area: Critical Infrastructure](critical-infrastructure.md) — Safety components in the management and operation of critical digital infrastructure, road traffic, and supply of water, gas, heating and electricity.
- [High-Risk Area: Education and Vocational Training](education-and-vocational-training.md) — AI systems used to determine access, admission, assignment, or evaluation of students and learners.
- [High-Risk Area: Employment and Workers Management](employment-and-workers-management.md) — Recruitment, screening, task allocation, performance evaluation, and promotion or termination decision-making.
- [High-Risk Area: Law Enforcement](law-enforcement.md) — Individual risk assessment of crime, polygraphs, evidence evaluation, profiling, and predictive analytics in criminal justice.
- [High-Risk Area: Migration, Asylum and Border Control](migration-asylum-and-border-control.md) — Polygraphs, examination of visa and asylum applications, security risk screening, and border surveillance.
