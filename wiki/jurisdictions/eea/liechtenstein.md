# Summary

Implementation, market surveillance structure, notifying authorities, and AI regulatory sandboxes for Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] in **Liechtenstein** (LI).

# National Authorities

## Market Surveillance Authority
Office for Communications (Amt für Kommunikation - AK) and Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzstelle - DSS).

## Notifying Authority
Office for Economic Affairs (Amt für Volkswirtschaft - AVW).

## Official Gazette
Liechtenstein National Gazette (Liechtensteinisches Landesgesetzblatt - LGBl.).

## AI Regulatory Sandbox
Coordinated with Swiss and EEA testbeds under the Liechtenstein Token and Trusted Technology Services Act.

# Legislative and Implementation Status
Pending EEA Joint Committee Decision for incorporation into the EEA Agreement.
