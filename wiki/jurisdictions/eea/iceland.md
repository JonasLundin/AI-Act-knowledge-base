# Summary

Implementation, market surveillance structure, notifying authorities, and AI regulatory sandboxes for Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] in **Iceland** (IS).

# National Authorities

## Market Surveillance Authority
Electronic Communications Office of Iceland (Fjarskiptastofa) and Data Protection Authority (Persónuvernd).

## Notifying Authority
Icelandic Accreditation Authority.

## Official Gazette
Legal Gazette (Stjórnartíðindi).

## AI Regulatory Sandbox
EEA sandbox collaboration with Nordic digital testbeds.

# Legislative and Implementation Status
