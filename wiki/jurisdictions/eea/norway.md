# Summary

Implementation, market surveillance structure, notifying authorities, and AI regulatory sandboxes for Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] in **Norway** (NO).

# National Authorities

## Market Surveillance Authority
Norwegian Digitalisation Agency (Digitaliseringsdirektoratet - Digdir) and Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

## Notifying Authority
Norwegian Accreditation (Norsk akkreditering).

## Official Gazette
Norwegian Law Gazette (Norsk Lovtidend).

## AI Regulatory Sandbox
Datatilsynet Regulatory Sandbox for Artificial Intelligence operational since 2020.

# Legislative and Implementation Status
Pending EEA Joint Committee Decision; national preparatory working group coordinating Digdir and Datatilsynet roles.
