# Summary

Implementation, market surveillance structure, notifying authorities, and AI regulatory sandboxes for Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] in **Denmark** (DK).

# National Authorities

## Market Surveillance Authority
Danish Business Authority (Erhvervsstyrelsen) and Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

## Notifying Authority
Danish Safety Technology Authority (Sikkerhedsstyrelsen).

## Official Gazette
Danish Legal Gazette (Lovtidende).

## AI Regulatory Sandbox
Datatilsynet regulatory sandbox for AI and data protection projects.

# Legislative and Implementation Status
National law establishing Erhvervsstyrelsen as lead market surveillance authority.
