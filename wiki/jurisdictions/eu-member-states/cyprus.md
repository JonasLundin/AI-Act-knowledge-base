Implementation, market surveillance structure, notifying authorities, and AI regulatory sandboxes for Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] in **Cyprus** (CY).

## Market Surveillance Authority

## Official Gazette
