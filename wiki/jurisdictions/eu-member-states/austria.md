# Summary

Implementation, market surveillance structure, notifying authorities, and AI regulatory sandboxes for Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] in **Austria** (AT).

# National Authorities

## Market Surveillance Authority
Austrian Regulatory Authority for Broadcasting and Telecommunications (RTR) and Federal Ministry for Climate Action, Environment, Energy, Mobility, Innovation and Technology (BMK).

## Notifying Authority
Federal Ministry for Labour and Economy (BMAW).

## Official Gazette
Federal Law Gazette (Bundesgesetzblatt für die Republik Österreich - BGBl.).

## AI Regulatory Sandbox
RTR AI Sandbox initiatives launched under the Austrian AI Strategy.

# Legislative and Implementation Status
National implementation bill coordinating supervisory competences between RTR and BMK.
