# Summary

Implementation, market surveillance structure, notifying authorities, and AI regulatory sandboxes for Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] in **Italy** (IT).

# National Authorities

## Market Surveillance Authority
Agency for Digital Italy (AgID) and National Cybersecurity Agency (ACN) designated as lead national authorities under the national AI Bill; Garante per la protezione dei dati personali supervising fundamental rights.

## Notifying Authority
Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy (MIMIT) / Accredia.

## Official Gazette
Official Gazette of the Italian Republic (Gazzetta Ufficiale della Repubblica Italiana).

## AI Regulatory Sandbox
AgID and Department for Digital Transformation regulatory sandbox for AI.

# Legislative and Implementation Status
National AI Bill (Disegno di Legge AI) establishing AgID and ACN competences approved by Council of Ministers.
