# Summary

Implementation, market surveillance structure, notifying authorities, and AI regulatory sandboxes for Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] in **Ireland** (IE).

# National Authorities

## Market Surveillance Authority
Data Protection Commission (DPC) and Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (CCPC).

## Notifying Authority
Irish National Accreditation Board (INAB).

## Official Gazette
Iris Oifigiúil.

## AI Regulatory Sandbox
Enterprise Ireland and DPC AI testing spaces.

# Legislative and Implementation Status
Inter-departmental working group preparing national legislation under the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment.
