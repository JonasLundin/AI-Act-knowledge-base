# Summary

Implementation, market surveillance structure, notifying authorities, and AI regulatory sandboxes for Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] in **Estonia** (EE).

# National Authorities

## Market Surveillance Authority
Consumer Protection and Technical Regulatory Authority (TTJA).

## Notifying Authority
Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications.

## Official Gazette
State Gazette (Riigi Teataja).

## AI Regulatory Sandbox
Estonian government AI sandbox and testbed environment ('Kratt' initiative).

# Legislative and Implementation Status
Designation of TTJA as the national supervisory authority under Article 70.
