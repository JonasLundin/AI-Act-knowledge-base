# Summary

Implementation, market surveillance structure, notifying authorities, and AI regulatory sandboxes for Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689] in **Poland** (PL).

# National Authorities

## Market Surveillance Authority
Ministry of Digital Affairs (Ministerstwo Cyfryzacji) proposing the Commission for AI Development and Safety (Komisja Rozwoju i Bezpieczenstwa Sztucznej Inteligencji - KRiBSI) alongside Office of Electronic Communications (UKE) and UODO.

## Notifying Authority
Polish Centre for Accreditation (PCA).

## Official Gazette
Journal of Laws of the Republic of Poland (Dziennik Ustaw).

## AI Regulatory Sandbox
Ministry of Digital Affairs regulatory sandbox initiative.

# Legislative and Implementation Status
Draft Act on Systems of Artificial Intelligence (Ustawa o systemach sztucznej inteligencji) establishing KRiBSI.
