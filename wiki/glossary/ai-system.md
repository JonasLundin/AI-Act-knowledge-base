# Definition

**AI System**: A machine-based system designed to operate with varying levels of autonomy and that may exhibit adaptiveness after deployment, inferring from inputs how to generate outputs.[^regulation-eu-2024-1689]

# Legal Context
Defined in Article 3 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.
