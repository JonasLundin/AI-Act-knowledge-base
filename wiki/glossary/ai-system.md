# Definition

**AI System**: Under the AI Act, an artificial intelligence system refers to an engineered computational mechanism capable of functioning with different degrees of independence from direct human instruction. For explicit or implicit objectives, it processes received inputs through inference models to produce content, predictions, recommendations, or decisions that influence physical or virtual environments (Article 3(1), aligned with the OECD revised definition)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Legal Context
Defined in Article 3, point (1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. This definition distinguishes AI systems from classical rule-based or purely algorithmic software programs.
