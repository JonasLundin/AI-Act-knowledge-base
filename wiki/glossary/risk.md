# Definition

**Risk**: Under the AI Act framework, risk denotes the joint consideration of how likely an adverse event or detrimental impact is to materialize, coupled with the degree of severity or magnitude of the resulting injury to health, safety, or Union fundamental rights (Article 3(2))[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Legal Context
Defined in Article 3, point (2) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Risk serves as the foundational metric underlying the four-tier categorization architecture of the AI Act.
