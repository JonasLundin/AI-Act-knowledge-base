# Summary

**Articles 72 and 73** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 establish the post-market surveillance architecture for high-risk AI systems[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Post-Market Monitoring Plan (Article 72)
Providers must establish a post-market monitoring system based on a formal plan (Annex IX), actively collecting, analyzing, and documenting real-world performance data to evaluate continuous conformity.

# Serious Incident Reporting Clock (Article 73)
Providers must report any **serious incident** to the market surveillance authorities of the affected Member States and the AI Office:
1. **General Reporting Window**: Immediately, and no later than **15 days** after becoming aware.
2. **Widespread Infringement or Critical Infrastructure Disruption**: No later than **2 days (48 hours)**.
3. **Death of a Person**: Immediately, and no later than **7 days**.

# Definition of Serious Incident (Article 3(49))
Incident that directly or indirectly leads to:
- Death of a person or serious damage to health;
- Serious and irreversible disruption of management or operation of critical infrastructure;
- Infringement of Union fundamental rights legislation;
- Serious damage to property or the environment.
