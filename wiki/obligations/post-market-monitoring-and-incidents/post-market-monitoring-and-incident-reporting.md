# Summary

**Articles 72 and 73** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 establish the post-market surveillance architecture for high-risk AI systems[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Post-Market Monitoring Plan (Article 72)
Providers of high-risk AI systems must establish and document a post-market monitoring system based on a formal post-market monitoring plan. Pursuant to **Article 72(3)**, the post-market monitoring plan forms an integral part of the technical documentation specified in **Annex IV**. The Commission is mandated to adopt an implementing act establishing a standard post-market monitoring template.

# Serious Incident Reporting Timelines (Article 73)
Providers must report any **serious incident** to the market surveillance authorities of the Member States where the incident occurred:
1. **General Reporting Window (Article 73(2))**: Immediately after the provider has established a causal link or the reasonable likelihood of such link, and in any event no later than **15 days** after becoming aware of the incident.
2. **Widespread Infringement or Critical Infrastructure Disruption (Article 73(3))**: Immediately, and no later than **2 days (48 hours)** after becoming aware.
3. **Death of a Person (Article 73(4))**: Immediately after establishing or suspecting a causal link, and no later than **10 days** after becoming aware of the incident.

# Definition of Serious Incident (Article 3(49))
Any incident or malfunctioning of an AI system that directly or indirectly leads to:
- Death of a person or serious damage to a person's health;
- Serious and irreversible disruption of the management or operation of critical infrastructure;
- Infringement of obligations under Union law protecting fundamental rights;
- Serious damage to property or the environment.
