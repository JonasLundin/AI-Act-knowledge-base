# Conformity assessment

Internal control versus notified-body routes, Annex VI and VII procedures, CE marking, declaration of conformity.

## Concepts

- [CE Marking and EU Declaration of Conformity (Articles 47-49)](ce-marking-and-declaration-of-conformity.md) — Mandates drawing up the EU Declaration of Conformity under Annex V and affixing the CE marking visibly and legibly.
- [Conformity Assessment Procedures (Article 43)](conformity-assessment-routes.md) — The two primary conformity assessment pathways: internal control (Annex VI) versus third-party notified body involvement (Annex VII).
