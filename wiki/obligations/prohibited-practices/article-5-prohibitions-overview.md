# Summary

**Article 5** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 establishes an absolute, unconditional ban across the European Union on eight categories of AI practices deemed unacceptable threats to human dignity, safety, and democracy[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

Infringements trigger the AI Act's highest administrative fine tier: up to **€35,000,000 or 7% of total worldwide annual turnover** (Article 99(3)).

# The 8 Prohibited Practices Matrix

1. **Subliminal / Deceptive Techniques (Article 5(1)(a))**: Deploying subliminal, purposefully manipulative, or deceptive techniques to materially distort human behavior, causing significant harm.
2. **Exploitation of Vulnerabilities (Article 5(1)(b))**: Exploiting age, disability, or specific socio-economic vulnerabilities to distort behavior and cause significant harm.
3. **Social Scoring (Article 5(1)(c))**: Evaluating or classifying natural persons over time based on social behavior or personality traits leading to detrimental or disproportionate treatment.
4. **Individual Predictive Policing (Article 5(1)(d))**: Making risk assessments on individuals to predict criminal offending based solely on profiling or personality traits.
5. **Untargeted Facial Recognition Scraping (Article 5(1)(e))**: Creating or expanding facial recognition databases through untargeted scraping of CCTV footage or internet images.
6. **Workplace & Educational Emotion Recognition (Article 5(1)(f))**: Inferring emotions of persons in workplaces or educational institutions (except for medical/safety purposes).
7. **Biometric Categorization of Sensitive Attributes (Article 5(1)(g))**: Categorizing individuals based on biometric data to deduce race, political opinions, trade union membership, religious/philosophical beliefs, sex life, or sexual orientation.
8. **Real-Time Remote Biometric Identification in Public Spaces (Article 5(1)(h))**: Real-time RBI by law enforcement in publicly accessible spaces, subject to strict judicial authorization exceptions (targeted search for kidnapping victims, imminent terror threats, specified severe crimes).
