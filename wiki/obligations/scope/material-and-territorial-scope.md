# Summary

**Article 2(1)** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 defines the extraterritorial scope of the AI Act[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

The AI Act applies not only to providers and deployers established within the European Union, but also to third-country providers and deployers where the output produced by the AI system is used in the Union.

# Territorial Application Matrix
1. **Providers**: Placing on the market or putting into service AI systems or GPAI models in the EU, irrespective of whether established in the EU or a third country.
2. **Deployers**: Having place of establishment or located within the EU.
3. **Third-Country Providers and Deployers**: Located outside the EU where the output produced by the system is intended to be used or is used in the EU.
4. **Importers & Distributors**: Making AI systems available on the EU market.
5. **Product Manufacturers**: Placing on the market or putting into service an AI system together with their product under Annex I legislation.
