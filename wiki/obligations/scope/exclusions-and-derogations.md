# Summary

**Article 2(3)–(12)** sets out explicit statutory exclusions removing specific AI activities from the scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Key Statutory Exclusions
1. **Military and Defense (Article 2(3))**: AI systems developed or used exclusively for military, defense, or national security purposes.
2. **Public Authorities in Third Countries (Article 2(5))**: Law enforcement / judicial cooperation under international agreements.
3. **Scientific Research and Development (Article 2(6))**: AI systems specifically developed and put into service for the sole purpose of scientific research and development.
4. **Pre-Market R&D and Prototyping (Article 2(8))**: Research, testing, and development activity regarding AI systems prior to placement on the market.
5. **Personal Non-Professional Use (Article 2(10))**: Purely personal, non-professional activities by natural persons.
6. **Open-Source AI (Article 2(12))**: AI systems released under free and open-source licenses, unless placed on the market as high-risk or prohibited systems.
