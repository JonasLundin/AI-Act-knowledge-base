# Summary

**Article 50(4)** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 mandates that deployers of an AI system that generates or manipulates image, audio, or video content constituting a **deepfake** must disclose that the content has been artificially generated or manipulated[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Definition and Disclosure Thresholds

### Deepfake Definition (Article 3(60))
An AI-generated or manipulated image, audio, or video that resembles existing persons, objects, places, entities, or events and would falsely appear to a person to be authentic or truthful.

### Mandatory Disclosure Requirements
1. **Prominent and Visible Labelling**: The disclosure must be timely, conspicuous, and persistently visible during the consumption of the media.
2. **Text of Public Interest**: Where AI generates or manipulates text published with the purpose of informing the public on matters of public interest, deployers must disclose that the text is artificially generated unless human editorial review was conducted.
3. **Exceptions for Artistic and Satirical Works**:
   - For artistic, creative, satirical, fictional, or parodic works, the disclosure duty is adapted so as not to hamper display, requiring appropriate labelling that indicates the synthetic nature without destroying the aesthetic work.
