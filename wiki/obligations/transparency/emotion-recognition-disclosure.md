# Summary

**Article 50(3)** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 mandates that deployers of emotion recognition systems or biometric categorization systems must inform natural persons exposed thereto of the operation of the system[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Statutory Obligations for Deployers

1. **Pre-Exposure Notification**: Individuals must be notified *before* or *at the moment* biometric data processing commences.
2. **GDPR Alignment**: Deployers must process personal data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Regulation (EU) 2018/1725, ensuring a valid lawful basis (e.g., explicit consent).
3. **Exceptions**:
   - AI systems used for biometric categorization and emotion recognition that are permitted by law to detect, prevent, or investigate criminal offences.

# Relationship to Article 5 Prohibitions
Deployers must verify that the emotion recognition system does not fall under **Article 5(1)(f)**, which categorically bans emotion recognition in the workplace and educational institutions except for medical or safety reasons.
