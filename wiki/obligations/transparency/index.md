# Transparency Obligations

Obligations governing user notification, synthetic content detection, watermarking, and deepfake labelling under Article 50 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act).

## Concepts

- [AI-Human Interaction Disclosure (Article 50(1))](ai-interaction-disclosure.md) — Mandatory obligation for providers to ensure AI systems interacting with natural persons inform them that they are interacting with AI.
- [Deepfake Labelling and Manipulated Media Disclosure (Article 50(4))](deepfake-labelling.md) — Obligates deployers of AI systems generating deepfakes or synthetic text of public interest to prominently disclose that the content is artificially generated.
- [Emotion Recognition and Biometric Categorization Disclosure (Article 50(3))](emotion-recognition-disclosure.md) — Mandates deployers to inform natural persons whenever they are exposed to emotion recognition or biometric categorization systems.
- [Synthetic Content Marking and Watermarking (Article 50(2))](synthetic-content-marking.md) — Mandates providers of generative AI systems to mark synthetic audio, image, video, and text in machine-readable formats.
