# Summary

**Article 50: Transparency Obligations** under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

Mandatory user disclosures for AI chatbots, biometric categorization, emotion recognition, and synthetic/deepfake content.

# Detailed Requirements
Statutory obligations imposed on economic operators placing systems on the market or putting them into service.
