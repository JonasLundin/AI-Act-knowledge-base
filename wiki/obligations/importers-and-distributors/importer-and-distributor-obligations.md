# Summary

**Articles 23, 24, and 25** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 govern the responsibilities of commercial intermediaries making AI systems available on the Union market[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Duties of Importers (Article 23)
- Verify provider carried out conformity assessment and drew up technical documentation.
- Verify system bears CE marking and instructions of use.
- Ensure storage or transport conditions do not jeopardize compliance.

# Duties of Distributors (Article 24)
- Verify CE marking, DoC, and instructions of use before making system available.
- Refrain from making non-compliant systems available; inform market surveillance authorities of risks.

# Circumstances Shifting Status to Provider (Article 25)
Any distributor, importer, deployer, or third party is legally considered a **Provider** and subject to all provider obligations if they:
1. Put their name or trademark on a high-risk AI system;
2. Make a substantial modification to a high-risk AI system;
3. Modify the intended purpose of an AI system such that it becomes high-risk.
