# Summary

**Article 53** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 sets out the horizontal baseline obligations applicable to all providers of **General-Purpose AI (GPAI) Models** placed on the EU market[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# The Four Baseline Duties (Article 53(1))
1. **Technical Documentation to AI Office (Point a)**: Maintain and provide technical documentation detailing training methodology, data sources, and evaluation results (Annex XI).
2. **Information for Downstream Providers (Point b)**: Provide documentation to downstream system integrators to enable them to comply with the AI Act (Annex XII).
3. **Copyright Policy (Point c)**: Implement a policy to comply with Union law on copyright and related rights, in particular respecting text and data mining opt-outs under Directive (EU) 2019/790 Article 4(3).
4. **Public Training Data Summary (Point d)**: Draw up and make publicly available a sufficiently detailed summary about the content used for training the GPAI model, following the template provided by the AI Office.

# Open-Source Exemption (Article 53(2))
GPAI models released under free and open-source licenses whose parameters, architecture, and weights are publicly available are exempt from points (a) and (b), provided they do not present systemic risk. Points (c) and (d) remain mandatory for all models.
