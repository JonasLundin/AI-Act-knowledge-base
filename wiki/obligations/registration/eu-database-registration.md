# Summary

Under **Article 49** and **Article 71** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, providers must register their high-risk AI systems listed in Annex III in the publicly accessible **EU Database for High-Risk AI Systems** managed by the Commission[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Mandatory Information Submitted (Annex VIII)
1. Name, address, and contact details of provider and authorized representative.
2. AI system trade name and commercial description.
3. Intended purpose and classification status under Annex III.
4. Electronic instructions for use and summary of conformity assessment.
5. Member States in which the system is placed on the market or put into service.
6. URL of the EU Declaration of Conformity.

# Deployer Registration Duties
Public authority deployers using high-risk AI systems must also register in the EU database pursuant to Article 49(2).
