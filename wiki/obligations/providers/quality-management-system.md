# Summary

**Article 17** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 requires providers of high-risk AI systems to put a quality management system (QMS) in place that ensures compliance with the regulation in a systematic and orderly manner[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Core Elements of the AI QMS
1. Strategy for regulatory compliance and conformity assessment procedures.
2. Design and development control procedures, data management techniques, and testing protocols.
3. Post-market monitoring system (Article 72) and serious incident handling (Article 73).
4. Resource allocation, accountability frameworks, and documentation management.
