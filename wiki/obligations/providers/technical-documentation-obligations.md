# Summary

Under **Article 16(c)** and **Article 11**, providers of high-risk AI systems must draw up the technical documentation referred to in Annex IV before that system is placed on the market or put into service, keeping it up to date[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Mandatory Annex IV Dossier Contents
1. General description of the AI system, intended purpose, and hardware requirements.
2. Detailed description of system architecture, algorithmic design, model specifications, training methodology, and data pipelines.
3. Risk management documentation under Article 9.
4. Validation and testing reports, test metrics, and bias evaluation results.
5. Cybersecurity controls, human oversight interfaces, and EU Declaration of Conformity.
