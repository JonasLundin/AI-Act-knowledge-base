# Obligations

Role and lifecycle views of the regulation's requirements.

## Structure

- [Scope](scope/index.md): Article 2 scope, the AI-system definition, exclusions, and the relationship to Annex I product legislation.
- [Prohibited practices](prohibited-practices/index.md): Article 5 practices and the Commission guidelines on them.
- [Providers](providers/index.md): High-risk provider duties: risk management, data governance, technical documentation, logging, transparency, human oversight, accuracy and robustness, quality management, conformity assessment, registration, corrective action.
- [Deployers](deployers/index.md): Article 26 duties, fundamental-rights impact assessment, human oversight, information to workers and affected persons.
- [Importers, distributors and authorised representatives](importers-and-distributors/index.md): Verification, cooperation and record duties along the value chain.
- [General-purpose AI](general-purpose-ai/index.md): GPAI model obligations, systemic-risk designation, the Code of Practice, downstream information duties.
- [Transparency](transparency/index.md): Article 50 disclosure, synthetic-content marking, deepfake labelling and their dates.
- [Conformity assessment](conformity-assessment/index.md): Internal control versus notified-body routes, Annex VI and VII procedures, CE marking, declaration of conformity.
- [Post-market monitoring and incidents](post-market-monitoring-and-incidents/index.md): Article 72 monitoring plans and Article 73 serious-incident reporting.
- [Registration](registration/index.md): The EU database for high-risk AI systems and its registration duties.
