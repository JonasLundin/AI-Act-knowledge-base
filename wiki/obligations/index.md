# Obligations

Role and lifecycle views of the regulation's requirements.

## Sections

- [Conformity assessment](conformity-assessment/index.md) — Internal control versus notified-body routes, Annex VI and VII procedures, CE marking, declaration of conformity.
- [Deployer Obligations](deployers/index.md) — Obligations applicable to deployers using high-risk AI systems in professional and commercial contexts.
- [General-Purpose AI (GPAI) Obligations](general-purpose-ai/index.md) — Obligations applicable to providers of general-purpose AI models, including systemic risk thresholds and downstream documentation.
- [Importers, distributors and authorised representatives](importers-and-distributors/index.md) — Verification, cooperation and record duties along the value chain.
- [Post-market monitoring and incidents](post-market-monitoring-and-incidents/index.md) — Article 72 monitoring plans and Article 73 serious-incident reporting.
- [Prohibited AI Practices](prohibited-practices/index.md) — Unconditional bans on AI practices that present unacceptable risks to health, safety, and fundamental rights.
- [Provider Obligations](providers/index.md) — Horizontal and high-risk obligations applicable to providers of AI systems placed on the market or put into service in the Union.
- [Registration](registration/index.md) — The EU database for high-risk AI systems and its registration duties.
- [Scope](scope/index.md) — Article 2 scope, the AI-system definition, exclusions, and the relationship to Annex I product legislation.
- [Transparency Obligations](transparency/index.md) — Obligations governing user notification, synthetic content detection, watermarking, and deepfake labelling under Article 50 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act).
