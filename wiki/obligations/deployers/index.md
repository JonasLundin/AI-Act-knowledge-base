# Deployer Obligations

Obligations applicable to deployers using high-risk AI systems in professional and commercial contexts.

## Concepts

- [Deployer Operational Obligations (Article 26)](deployer-operational-obligations.md) — Statutory requirements for deployers of high-risk AI: instructions of use, human oversight, input data controls, and logging.
- [Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment - FRIA (Article 27)](fundamental-rights-impact-assessment.md) — Mandates public bodies and certain private deployers to conduct an impact assessment on fundamental rights prior to putting high-risk AI into service.
