# Summary

**Article 27** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 mandates that prior to putting a high-risk AI system into service, deployers that are bodies governed by public law, or private operators providing public services, must conduct an assessment of the impact on fundamental rights (**FRIA**)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# FRIA Structure and Assessment Steps
1. **Deployment Context**: Describe deployer processes and specific period of time during which the system will be used.
2. **Affected Groups**: Identify specific categories of natural persons and vulnerable groups likely to be affected.
3. **Fundamental Rights Risks**: Assess risks of discrimination, privacy infringement, due process denial, or harm to human dignity.
4. **Human Oversight Measures**: Detail the human oversight measures implemented to mitigate identified harms.
5. **Notification**: Transmit the completed FRIA to the relevant national market surveillance authority.
