# Summary

**Article 26** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 sets out the mandatory obligations incumbent on **deployers** of high-risk AI systems[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Key Deployer Duties
1. **Adherence to Instructions**: Deploy and use the system strictly in accordance with the provider's instructions for use (Article 26(1)).
2. **Human Oversight Assignment**: Assign natural persons who possess the necessary competence, training, authority, and support to exercise human oversight (Article 26(2)).
3. **Input Data Relevance**: Ensure input data is relevant and sufficiently representative in view of the intended purpose (Article 26(4)).
4. **Continuous Monitoring & Incident Notification**: Suspend use and notify provider/authorities if an incident or unacceptable risk occurs (Article 26(5)).
5. **Log Retention**: Automatically generated logs must be retained for at least 6 months (Article 26(6)).
6. **Worker Information**: Inform workers' representatives and affected employees before putting high-risk AI into service in the workplace (Article 26(7)).
