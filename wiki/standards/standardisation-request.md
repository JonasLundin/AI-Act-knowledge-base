# Summary

**Commission Standardisation Requests** form the legal basis for the development of European harmonised standards conferring presumption of conformity under **Article 40** of the AI Act[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Mandate Evolution: M/593 to M/613
- **Initial Request (M/593)**: Adopted under Implementing Decision C(2023) 3215[^commission-decision-c-2023-3215] in May 2023 based on the Commission's original legislative proposal.
- **Revised Request (M/613)**: Adopted under Implementing Decision C(2025) 3871[^commission-decision-c-2025-3871] in June 2025 to reflect the final adopted text of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 and set a binding deadline of **28 February 2027** for CEN and CENELEC to deliver ten core harmonised European standards.

# The Ten Mandated Deliverables
1. Risk management system for AI;
2. Governance and quality of datasets;
3. Record-keeping and automatic logging;
4. Transparency and information provision to deployers;
5. Human oversight tools and measures;
6. Accuracy metrics and validation methodologies;
7. Robustness against errors and operational disruptions;
8. Cybersecurity for AI systems;
9. Quality management systems for providers of high-risk AI;
10. Conformity assessment procedures for high-risk AI.
