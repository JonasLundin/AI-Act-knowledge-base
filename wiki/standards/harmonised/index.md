# Harmonised Standards

European harmonised standards developed by CEN and CENELEC under standardisation requests M/593 and M/613 conferring presumption of conformity under Article 40.

## Status Overview

As of late 2026, **no harmonised standards have been cited in the Official Journal of the European Union (OJEU)**. Drafting of European standardisation deliverables is currently underway within Joint Technical Committee CEN/CLC/JTC 21.

Under Commission Implementing Decision C(2025) 3871 (M/613), the delivery deadline for the ten mandated harmonised standards is **28 February 2027**. Once published in the OJEU, compliance with these standards will confer a legal presumption of conformity with the corresponding requirements of Chapter III, Section 2 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.

## Concepts

- [Commission Standardisation Requests (M/593 & M/613)](../standardisation-request.md) — Legal mandate C(2025) 3871 establishing deliverables and delivery deadline of 28 February 2027.
- [CEN/CENELEC JTC 21](../committees/cen-cenelec-jtc-21.md) — Joint Technical Committee responsible for developing European harmonised AI standards.
