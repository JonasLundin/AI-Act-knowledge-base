# Summary

**CEN-CENELEC Joint Technical Committee 21 (JTC 21)** is the official European standardisation body tasked by the European Commission with developing harmonised European standards supporting the AI Act[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Standardisation Request M/593
Under Commission Standardisation Request M/593, JTC 21 develops European standards covering:
- AI Quality Management Systems (harmonising ISO/IEC 42001)
- AI Risk Management (harmonising ISO/IEC 23894)
- AI Data Quality and Governance
- Technical Robustness and Cybersecurity of AI
- Human Oversight, Transparency, and Explainability
