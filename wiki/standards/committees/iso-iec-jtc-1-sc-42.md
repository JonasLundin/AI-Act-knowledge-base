# Summary

**ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42** is the international standardisation subcommittee developing horizontal, cross-industry foundational standards for artificial intelligence[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Relationship with European Harmonisation
Under the Vienna Agreement between ISO and CEN, CEN/CLC/JTC 21 assesses and fast-tracks relevant SC 42 deliverables (such as ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 23894, and ISO/IEC 5338) as European Standards (EN ISO/IEC), adapting them to satisfy specific European regulatory requirements.
