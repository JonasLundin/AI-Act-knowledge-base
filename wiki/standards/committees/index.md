# Standardisation Committees

European and international standardisation committees developing AI standards.

## Concepts

- [CEN/CENELEC JTC 21](cen-cenelec-jtc-21.md) — Joint Technical Committee responsible for European harmonised AI standards.
- [ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 Artificial Intelligence](iso-iec-jtc-1-sc-42.md) — International standardisation subcommittee developing horizontal foundational AI standards.
- [ETSI Technical Committee on Artificial Intelligence](etsi.md) — European Standards Organisation addressing network, telecommunications, and cybersecurity AI standards.
