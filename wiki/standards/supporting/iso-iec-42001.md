# Summary

**ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Management system)** is the international standard specifying requirements for an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS)[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Mapping to AI Act Article 17
ISO/IEC 42001 provides organizations with a structured management system that directly satisfies Article 17 QMS obligations:
- AI policy and governance leadership
- Risk assessment and AI impact assessments
- Data management and provenance controls
- Continuous post-market monitoring and corrective action
