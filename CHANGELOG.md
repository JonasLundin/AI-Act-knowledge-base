# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-27

### Added
- Comprehensive knowledge base covering Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act) as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI).
- Dedicated amendments page for Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 with change table.
- Extended timeline milestones including deferred high-risk application dates (2027-12-02 and 2028-08-02), Article 50 transparency application, Omnibus entry into force, and standardisation deadlines.
- Interacting legislation concepts: CRA Article 12, Product Liability Directive 2024/2853, Machinery Regulation 2023/1230, MDR/IVDR, and New Legislative Framework.
- Institutional governance pages: Notified Bodies, Market Surveillance Authorities, and AI Office.
- Standardisation architecture covering standardisation requests C(2023) 3215 and C(2025) 3871, CEN/CENELEC JTC 21, ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42, ETSI, and supporting standards (ISO/IEC 42001, 23894, 5338, 24027).
- Detailed national implementation pages for 27 EU Member States and 3 EEA countries with designated authorities, national sandboxes, and gazette citations.

### Changed
- Aligned Article 5 prohibited practices to 10 statutory prohibitions including new points (ba) and (bb) under Regulation (EU) 2026/1744.
- Consolidated Article 113 date table and Article 111 transitional provisions.
- Corrected Article 99 administrative fines (1% tier, Member State enforcement, Article 99(4) enumeration, and Article 99(6a) SME/medium-sized caps).
- Corrected post-market monitoring and serious incident reporting deadlines (10 days for death under Article 73(4), Annex IV plan under Article 72(3), and reporting to Member State market surveillance authorities).
- Paraphrased verbatim statutory definitions in glossary.
- Normalised all concept frontmatters to standard OKF types and differentiated stale_after dates.
- Rebuilt sources and coverage registers with verified primary legal acts and standardisation mandates.
